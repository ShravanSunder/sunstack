1. How sure must Jev be of a skill's kind?

Each skill gets one kind: general, role, domain, tool_manual or repo_specific. The three general-skill rules only judge general, role and domain skills. Today's rule (yours, 3 Nov): if Jev isn't at least 70% sure of the exact kind, the skill is skipped and listed.

The catch, with a made-up example. Jev answers for one skill:

KindJev's probabilityDo the general-skill rules judge this kind?
general36%yes
role34%yes
domain24%yes
tool_manual3%no
repo_specific3%no

No single kind reaches 70%, so today the skill is skipped, even though 94% of Jev's answer says "a kind these rules judge".
