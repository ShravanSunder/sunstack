Design pages state the author's assumptions and decisions

The updated design skills instruct the author of a design to mark each assumption and decision as it writes it, saying who it came from: you, an option you picked, a contract, or the agent itself. A reviewer then asks of every sentence that settles meaning whether its source really supports it. Only the few high-value calls come to you; the rest stay marked where you can read them.

Where it stands: the design passed its independent review. The skill files are written and committed on the branch, not yet reviewed or merged. Whether the agent really notices its assumptions while writing is the design's hypothesis; it has not been tested yet.

Who does what

Each check is a nudge: it says what it saw and where to look, and the author or reviewer decides. Nothing new blocks a design from being finished.

A real example

On 13 November you picked option b in a question an agent had written: "Every rule, J1 and the core-skill rules alike, gets candidates, then a Jev selection, then units the same way." The agent later wrote, in its own account: "I read your choice of option b as "the model picks the boundaries"." The Lead reports that this reading then went into a Requirements draft as your decision.

In that option, J1 names one of the rules being designed (the option does not define it further), and a Jev selection means Jev, the model the sunstack rules use for judgment questions, picks among candidate pieces of text.

Before: one sentence, credited to you, saying the model picks the boundaries.

After: two marked items.

Every document rule gets its evidence the same way. The owner selected the option worded:

Every rule, J1 and the core-skill rules alike, gets candidates, then a Jev selection, then units the same way.

I read "a Jev selection" as Jev choosing where units start and end. Ranked high: it settles your meaning, so it comes to you as a question.

Depends on it: shortened for this page. A full assumption item links the obligations that rely on it; the complete shape is in the Specification.

What changes, for whom

  • The author marks each assumption and decision in the same edit as the sentence, and writes "unknown" rather than leaving it out.
  • The reviewer asks of every sentence that settles meaning whether its source supports all of it, before reading the author's own check, and treats pages agreeing with each other as no evidence.
  • You get the few high-value calls as decisions, and one ranked list of everything else on the design's front page.

Details

Each item is a plain HTML aside with an id (A1, D1), its kind and its authority: owner-words, owner-accepted, contract, author or unknown. A shared table of ten nudge labels says what a check saw. Ranking asks whose meaning an item settles, how hard it is to undo, and how much rests on it. The shape lives in the shared design reference of sunstack-dev 3.3.0; the four skills that apply it are design-specification, design-program, design-verification and design-review in sunstack-workflow-v2 0.3.0. No new Demistify element: you decided that on 13 November.
