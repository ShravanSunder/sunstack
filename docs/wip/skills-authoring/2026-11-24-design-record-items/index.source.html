Design records: teach the skills the approved format

This proposal brings one shared reference and five skills in line with the Demistify record format the owner approved on 24 November 2026. Decisions become choices (when alternatives were weighed) or rulings (when none were), and an agent's decision names the agent, its role and a weight. A need, rule, realization or planned check stays beside the prose that explains it; every decision and assumption sits in a section item, and prose links to it. The skills stop naming old authority states and point at one home, design-documents.md.

Revision 2, after design review 1 (16 findings corrected). Open: two owner decisions, what counts as design weight (O1) and who records an agent's decision (O2); no behaviour has been evaluated. The skill pull request merges only after the Demistify release that ships the format.
