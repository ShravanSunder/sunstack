Show me your work: who does what

The third design document: how the skills, the Router log and Demistify make the Specification true. Nothing here is built yet. The Requirements are the first page.

  • Logging stays a ready Router command, filled in for each helper when I hand it the job. It's two plain steps: make two ids for the row, then write. The helper adds only what kind of row, who, and the words. A retry reuses the same ids, and Router then returns the first row instead of writing a second. I tested that once, with a resend after the first had finished, on this machine. A resend while the first is still on its way isn't tested yet; the planned check sends two at once to cover it. No new tool. If helpers get it wrong or skip rows, I'll build a short sunstack log command instead.
  • Each agent audits its own stretch before it hands work back, and I audit mine at each checkpoint. The reviews we already run are the second reader. If a review or you find something an agent's own audit missed, I'll add a second auditor.
  • The numbers at the top open their items as diagram boxes, the one thing Demistify can make clickable today. Try it below.

The other changes: under this design, every helper would log its own rows, even reviewers and evaluators who can't post on a thread; today those two roles only log when they may post. And your page would show each workstream (plain names like "Requirements" or "PR #194") with what's done, what's next, and the checks behind it.
