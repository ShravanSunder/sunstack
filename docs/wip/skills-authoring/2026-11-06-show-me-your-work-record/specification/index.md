# Specification: show me your work

The Specification entry: what must be observably true for each requirement in the [Requirements](../requirements/index.md) (U1–U12), and what decides pass or fail. How the skill and its tools make it true is the [Program Design](../program-design.md)'s; how each obligation is shown to hold on a real run is the [Verification Design](../verification-design.md)'s.

Its chunks, in reading order:

1. [Entities](entities.md): the nouns every obligation is written over, and what makes two of each the same. Every capability links here.
2. [Obligations](obligations.md): R1–R24 grouped by capability, what decides pass and fail (V1–V14), and coverage.
3. [The standards the skill states](skill-standards.md): S1–S12, one sentence each, and each rule with its two transfer cases.
