Show me your work: what I'll fix

You want to know the decisions, the assumptions, the shortcuts and what verification was done, so you can trust the agents' decisions and see what was missed. The skill says the right things, but the log agents keep under it carries much less and was often written late, so the page shows mostly what I did. This design fixes the log first, then what you see.

  • The point: "eys and i knwo what was deiceded and hwat is goign on".
  • Two parts: the skill is the log; a reference shows it to you with Demistify. They connect through how the agents log.
  • Every agent logs its own work as it goes, not from memory later; I put it together and own it. A peer Lead logs in its own project.
  • The log is Router's work trail, not a CSV or JSONL file: Router stamps who wrote each row and when. You needn't read it raw. How agents log is my call: easy and cheap for them. The rest of the design is the display.
  • The layout that tells you what to pay attention to and what to do stays.

Where this stands: all four design documents are written and independently reviewed, and the design is ready for planning. The other three: Specification (what must be true, with a sketch of your page), Program Design (who does what, with the clickable top working) and Verification Design (how each requirement will be shown). Nothing gets built until you say go.

In this record, few entries are the other agents' assumptions or changes from the design, no entry is tagged as a Lead acceptance, and the parts don't name pieces of a design. That's why every page view I built felt off: it was a better window onto what was mostly my own log.

How I'd fix it

  • One short command writes a row, so an agent logs the moment something happens: a decision, an assumption, a shortcut or a check.
  • Every commission carries the log's address, and the helper logs its own rows there; I don't accept a handback whose rows are missing.
  • Anything only you can settle is logged as waiting for you the moment it comes up.
  • At the end of a run, the agent checks its rows against what it actually did and logs what it missed, and the independent reviews we already run flag weak evidence and skipped checks. Together, these feed "what was missed".
  • The page opens on what needs your attention and what to do, then shows the decisions, assumptions, shortcuts and verification, grouped by a plain workstream label instead of "pieces".

The Program Design settles each of these; where it chose differently, it says so.
