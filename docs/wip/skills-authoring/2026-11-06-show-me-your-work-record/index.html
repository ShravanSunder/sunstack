In this record, few entries are the other agents' assumptions or changes from the design, no entry is tagged as a Lead acceptance, and the parts don't name pieces of a design. That's why every page view I built felt off: it was a better window onto what was mostly my own log.

How I'd fix it

  • Every commission carries the record and the pieces of the design the helper works on. The helper writes its own assumptions, decisions, departures and proof there.
  • The Lead doesn't accept a handback that's missing them, and every acceptance is recorded at its commit.
  • When work starts, the Lead writes the design's pieces into the record, and every entry names one.
  • An assumption only you can settle is written as waiting for you the moment it's made.
  • The page shows each piece with what each agent assumed, decided, broke and proved for it. It's like the Q2 flow, but the pieces come from the design instead of from my topics.

The next two design documents settle this: what must be true, then who does what. Two questions stay open for you after the first: what a piece is when the work has no design, and whether a peer Lead's entries from its own project belong here.
