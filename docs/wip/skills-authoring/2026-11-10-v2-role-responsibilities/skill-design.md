## Practices, principles and domains

Retain decision-relevant existing calls, not a blanket checklist. Lead uses collaboration, research, clarification, scope/model refinement and evidence acceptance where applicable. Implementer uses language standards, real tests, verification, scope and model refinement. Verifier uses verification/verification maintenance and source investigation for apparatus and observations. Checker uses its review procedure, source inspection and test-quality judgment. Operator uses its prescribed procedure/tool manual and its commissioner's record, without acquiring engineering acceptance duties.

Each touched call is inspected for condition, destination, work and useful result. Citations retain their observable decision and action. Base-root lookup remains the owner-approved cross-plugin exception to the generic same-plugin citation standard; it does not authorize copying base files. Neither a shorter role file nor a shorter handback permits skipping a practice's actual work. The currently advertised base is 3.1.1: its show-me-your-work retains logging/audit duties but explicitly removes automatic HTML/Story rendering at checkpoints. Do not restore the obsolete page ceremony from 3.0.0 or claim unchanged logging duties disappeared. Its preflight/verification still contain v1 callbacks, so the activation limit remains.

## Reference and reporting design

Main role files keep the responsibility, routing rule, required order and completion boundary in view. Existing relationship references own role-specific decisions and examples; pairing owns expected-outcome authority and stable targets once. Management owns assignment/bootstrap mechanics. Acceptance detail, if split out, belongs under role-lead/references, never a v2 shared-references folder.

At initial assignment, recipients load their role and current governing files, establish grants and stopping/ending conditions, and validate necessary context internally. Later role/files corrections still open the current referenced governing files and any source to be edited in full; unchanged preambles and the whole trace are not reread ceremonially. Return exact gaps, not a narrated packet. No external message/schema enforcement is claimed.

Handback states delivered outcome, target/diff, decisive evidence, unresolved gaps/holds and actual decisions needed. Link to the Verifier's original report rather than copying it. Product-test legwork remains required; report departures and deciding cases rather than duplicating a per-test narrative and a row-ID inventory. Preserve any mandatory result that an unchanged owning procedure independently requires.

The Lead completes acceptance against the original intent, design, observed behavior, integrated outcome and resolved prerequisites, then records its own judgment in the existing Lead acceptance record before commissioning independent delivery review. That record names the accepted scope and the still-pending independent review, so it does not falsely close delivery or grant merge authority. Run 2 teaches the five concrete acceptance conditions and the S11–S12 procedure in the existing working-with-implementer/review references or one coherent accepting-work.md procedure with a main-path caller; those callers keep order, required judgment and completion visible. Run 1 points to that Lead-owned procedure rather than redefining it.

Run 4 review-duty.md and review-with-lead.md teach an independent basis for judgment: derive criteria from original governing requirements/decisions and compare the implementation and Lead's assessment against them. The Lead's review caller makes the originals and assessment reachable without promoting the assessment into a governing premise. The explicit assessment is an artifact under review, not an authoritative brief or replacement source. Initial role/files, required reads and the shared record may expose it; recipient-startup's full reads and the base's history reads remain intact. R3 drops the agent-added guarantee that the Checker has not seen the assessment when forming questions, rather than adding record partitions, new wire fields or a staged second commission. No blind-review benefit is claimed. Missing original sources remains an input gap.

Implementer/Verifier reports remain evidence, not the Lead's own authored judgment. Findings go to the Lead for evidence-based disposition; repairs remain Implementer work. A finding reopens only the affected acceptance and proof, which the Lead checks before the Checker rechecks those corrections. A clean independent review is the last quality gate, with no second wholesale Lead acceptance phase afterward. No compulsory extra acceptance document, schema, row type or new state service is introduced.

R7 makes the acceptance artifact mandatory in the existing work record/governing files, rather than requiring a separate new document. The Lead's caller and the Reviewer's delivery procedure both teach S17 admission. The gate lives before full implementation review in review-duty/review-with-lead and the selected delivery-review procedure; it must not be hidden only in a Lead reminder. Inputs are original full governing sources and proof, not the Lead's frame. The Reviewer returns missing Lead work without doing that work on its behalf. Existing role/files commissioning points to those sources; no new wire field or loader is necessary.

Lead cadence belongs in its workstream relationships and management lifecycle depth, not in base-owned practices. Current advertised Router supports project/thread subscriptions with poll/deliver modes and durable interval wakes; an interval of 3300 seconds is expressible. Use one owner-selected 55-minute wake against the retained Lead and board status as the source, without a second short polling/push-wake loop. Inspect any existing wake/subscription before reuse/change, verify the saved policy when configured, use a bounded active-work lifetime and stop it on completion/pause. This proposal does not create or claim a live wake or cache benefit. Immediate material blocker/HOLD delivery is retained as the routine-cadence interpretation.
