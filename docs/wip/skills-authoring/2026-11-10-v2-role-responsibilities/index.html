Your correction: the 🐒 Implementer, 🔬 Verifier and 🔎 Reviewer are always separate Sidekicks. Subagents are helpers the Implementer may use. They do not replace those roles.

Who does the work, and who accepts it?

The Lead must satisfy all five conditions below before delivery review. Known missing work returns to the pair. A clean independent review does not start another acceptance ceremony.

The Lead records its own judgment in the existing work record. The Checker tests that judgment against original requirements. This is a proposed skill procedure, not software enforcement.

The Reviewer refuses an incomplete handoff

  • Lead's acceptance artifact: completed checklist, its own judgment and the evidence it inspected.
  • Complete governing material: original owner decisions, current specs/design, workstream plan and verification requirements for the assigned scope.
  • Actual candidate and proof: target/diff, original Verifier report, deciding runs and explicit unresolved-items state.

Missing acceptance, required proof or governing detail means review not started. The Reviewer returns the missing-input list to Lead. It does not complete Lead acceptance or basic verification on Lead's behalf.

One routine check-in every 55 minutes

Routine board updates accumulate for the check-in; the pair keeps working directly. Use one periodic trigger, not frequent status polls or duplicate push-wakes. Material blockers and HOLDs still reach Lead promptly. Stop the cadence when work completes or pauses.

The 55-minute cadence is selected. Keeping cache warm is its intent, not a measured guarantee. No live wake is configured by this page.

Parallel work belongs to the Implementer

The Implementer chooses helpers and execution steps without asking the Lead about each one. It reads their changes, integrates and proves the result. Ordinary fixes stay with Implementer and Verifier; mental-model breaks and questions they cannot settle go to Lead.

Sidekick: separate persistent session. Subagent: native helper under the Implementer. Worker remains only a search synonym. The primary Implementer, Verifier and Reviewer never become subagents.

The Verifier gives the Lead a full, actionable report

It challenges whether tests would detect the relevant failure, checks real boundaries and independently observes the required behavior. It sends defects straight to Implementer, rechecks fixes and keeps unsupported outcomes visible. A full report can be blocked; it does not mean everything passed.

When should an agent bring a question back?

Operators follow their prescribed procedure and return exceptions to their commissioner. The working pair cannot change expected behavior by agreement.
