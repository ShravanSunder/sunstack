Decisions go to the owner

A decision an agent raises itself is checked for authority first: anything on a line the owner owns, or outside the agent's authority, goes up the chain. Inside that authority, a two-way door is the agent's call: it decides, writes it in the trace and keeps going. A one-way door goes up the chain as the full brief: a helper to whoever assigned it, the Lead to the owner, and with no Lead, or when delivery to the Lead fails, straight to the owner; a Lead that hasn't answered yet is still pending. The Chief of Staff route, and the rule that held a brief's options back from the owner, are removed from the shared brief and the two skills that repeated them.

Four files in three named runs, plus a contract test for the maintained route. The owner ruled on the door split on 27 Nov; accepted to implement after six review rounds.

  2. Specification: the new routing rule and the exact new text.
  3. Skill design: the three runs.
  4. Skill verification: the checks.
