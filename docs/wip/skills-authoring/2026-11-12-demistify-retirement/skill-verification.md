The classification follows the decision matrix: literal supported-surface consistency is deterministic; author choice/reference use is work observed by an eval; API-preservation behavior is exercised by product tests; reviewer independence and claim provenance need source/run history and are review-only. The existing teaching lens is prose, not a new proof gate.

## Fresh author and packaging checks

Before the fresh author starts, freeze its realistic request and judging checks in a separate evidence folder outside its working directory. Give it only the actual matching guide/binding and ordinary source facts for a small grouped design explanation including a supplied call path. Do not name the retired component or reveal the checks. Use a fresh session with no authoring history; inspect its actual load/tool record and output, not its self-report. Check source ownership, supported components, complete shared navigation and final matching CLI result. Report only that named observation, with no RED/GREEN or causal improvement claim.

The named fresh author observation checks reference application through its actual authored/prepared output. Runtime call-stack interaction is separately proved by the retained product fixture and paired Verifier; the author observation does not stand in for that product behavior. No causal improvement is claimed.

Run skill-creation's rule pass on final edited skill files. Keep exact reports, accounts and any missing credential/tool gap; `rules: not run` is not green. Run applicable OXC/text/link/packaging and both-client plugin validations after the last edit, with the actual checks and exit codes. Run independent implementation review after proof and diff assessment. Exact client-trigger trials are not applicable because descriptions and invocation settings do not change.

## Limits and shipping

Until observed, fresh-author behavior is not evaluated. Static source checks do not establish comprehension or rendered quality. No old-session causal fix is claimed. Existing public assets and installed caches remain separate delivery states; prepare matching artifacts, but merge/publication/cache refresh need their own authority. Story experiments remain deferred until removal is finished.
