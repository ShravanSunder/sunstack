# 2026-10-18 Blameless retrospective

`sunstack-dev` 2.87.0 → 2.88.0.

- New practice `practices-retrospective` ("Blameless retrospective"). It looks back at a finished or bounded session or delivery from its actual records, explains each consequential outcome by its conditions rather than blame, opens the existing owner or check before proposing anything, and returns the smallest justified disposition as data: preserve a technique with its conditions, correct execution, a skill finding for `skill-audit`, a repository or check finding (including a broken fixture or asset), verification work for `practices-verification` or `practices-verification-maintenance` by predicate, a disputed expectation for its source owner, an investigation question, or no change.
- A finding authorizes reporting only. Proposed, implemented and proven stay separate, and the account carries reusable judgment, never private transcript bodies or secret values. Correcting an expected result to its adopted source is maintenance; weakening proof or hiding a regression never is.
- AGENTS.md lists the new practice in the skill table and the layer list; the sunstack-dev README gains its namespace entry and paragraph.
