# 2026-11-27: Work-log pages use four question sections and a full account

- `sunstack-dev` 3.10.0; the three plugin manifests and the three marketplaces agree.
- `presentation-show-worklog` builds the owner-approved work-log view: four question sections (For you to decide; What's done / what's next; What changed and why; What was verified, and what was missed) with fixed tones and icons, one item per topic with an `h4` face, and "a frame, not a form": a topic with no evidence for a badge, Flow or cards is plain prose, and no topic is dropped.
- New `references/section-views.md` teaches each section's view and the evidence that earns it: linked open choices with consequence badges only when the source states what waits, Flows only for stated dependencies, before and after cards only when both states are recorded, and one verification table on the shared `data-layout="verification"` marker with Passed, Failed, Blocked/Held, Not run and Observed labels. The view compares no alternatives and carries no answer control.
- New `references/full-account.md` teaches the companion full account the view links into when no stable account at the same cutoff exists: every row in the declared snapshot, unabridged, under the view's four section headings, one `entry-<row-id>` anchor each, row text rendered as text.
- `read-and-summarize.md` accepts an explicitly supplied dated report or full account as a source, and returns a claim inventory of every recorded check.
- `presentation-html`: a calling skill's page contract decides the forms it names; `presentation-html` chooses the rest and composes within it (`SKILL.md` line 10, and the work-account and options loads ask only for the forms a contract leaves open).
- Basis: the owner's approval of the 26 Nov view; design set and reviews in `docs/wip/skills-authoring/2026-11-27-worklog-four-sections/`.
- Companion links: a link to a row in the snapshot points at its anchor; any other link becomes clickable only for an `http` or `https` destination, and every other destination shows as text.
- Reviews: design review (round 1 targeted revision, closed) and implementation review (round 1 targeted revision, 20 findings corrected), each by independent reviewers of two lineages.
- Proof: four rounds of four fresh-author runs (OpenAI Terra high, Claude Opus high) on a frozen dated report and a frozen 74-row trail slice, then a narrow retest on a 75-row slice with an unsafe-link row. Every round kept the four sections, the full account with all rows and inert row markup. At the final full round all four pages passed the released 1.41.1 check and the 390 px Chrome check, and both Opus runs passed every hand check; earlier rounds had a 4 px inline-code overflow (theme fix pending), an older local CLI and missing row-header scope. Both Terra runs still miss some rules the skill states. The narrow retest on the corrected skill passed the companion, markup and unsafe-link checks for both authors (the `javascript:` destination stayed text), but neither page fully reconciled its verification table: Terra linked a corrected result to its last row instead of the correction's Trace and wrote the view before the companion, and Opus labelled a structural rename check as showing no change in meaning and dropped one fixture-repair result. The claim is "observed in named examples", with that variance named. Not verified: installed activation (runs loaded an exported copy), presentation-html's fallback for forms a contract leaves open, physical iPhone or Safari, external links.
