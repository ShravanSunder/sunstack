# Work-log pages explain agents' decisions and assumptions

- `sunstack-dev` **3.3.2** updates `presentation-show-worklog` (SKILL.md and `references/read-and-summarize.md`) and `presentation-html` (`references/patterns.md`).
- A requested work-log page explains each material decision agents made in its main account: the choice, who made it, its reason and material cost, and the authority that covered it or the gap. Missing rationale or cost evidence is named, not invented.
- Relied-on assumptions appear with the work that depends on them, the evidence so far and what would settle them; later corrections, withdrawals and settlements stay current, and incomplete source coverage is a named gap.
- Reconciliation explicitly includes agents' core choices and separates who chose from the authority that covered the choice. Its source-to-episode example adds one agent choice and one unproved dependency.
- `patterns.md` adds one worked work account showing which form does which job (native table, progress part only for a known count, comparison, Trace, disclosure) and when to omit a progress, comparison or sequence view.
- Unchanged: descriptions and invocation settings, the four page headings, request-only HTML, read-only history and component APIs; no call site is added, moved or renamed, though the evidence-reading call now also returns the material decisions and assumptions. The matching Demistify guide still owns syntax.
- [Validation, proof runs and limits](references/2026-11-13-worklog-component-teaching.md). Installed caches are not refreshed.
