# 2026-11-27: Decisions go up the chain to the owner; no Chief of Staff

- `sunstack-dev` 3.9.1, `sunstack-workflow-v2` 0.9.1; all plugin manifests and the three marketplaces agree.
- The shared `owner-decision-brief.md` drops the Chief of Staff route, the recipient-gap fallback and "keep the brief unsent". A decision an agent raises itself is checked for authority first: a choice on a line the owner owns, or outside the agent's delegated authority, goes up the chain however reversible it is. Inside that authority, a two-way door is the agent's call (decide, record it in the trace, keep going) and a one-way door goes up the chain.
- Up the chain means the full brief to whoever commissioned the agent: the Lead briefs the owner; a helper returns it to its commissioner; a helper the owner assigned directly goes to the work's Lead, or briefs the owner when no Lead is named or delivery to it failed. A queued, unanswered or uncertain message stays pending. A commissioner passes the brief on with its options and recommendation intact; only the owner decides an owner decision. A question tool asks only the owner, after the full brief.
- `practices-show-me-your-work` stop treatments 1 and 2, `design-program`'s `decision-needed` stop and `domain-code-standards` (one conditional load covering every standards case that needs the owner) deliver to the recipient the brief names instead of restating a route.
- New `tests/skills/lib/contracts/owner-decision-routing-contract.test.ts` checks the maintained route; the frozen v1 route test is unchanged.
- Basis: owner decision (no Chief of Staff for now) and owner ruling on one-way and two-way doors, 27 Nov.
