# Check: Chunk and Coverage

This check owns what the rule reads: whether its chunk is named with its edges, chosen for a reason, limited to the folders its standard governs, honest about what it didn't read, and repaired where the text is collected. Whether a sampled state holds the decisive content each question needs is `decision-model-review`'s state-and-evidence check; this one reads the chunk's design and its denominator.

**Inputs:** the rule's or selection's chunk design (subject, denominator, edges, fields, budget, and the reason for its chunk choice); the evidence kind and any `select`, `within` or filter gate in the config; the collection over the denominator, with its file counts, from one of two sources:
- **a prepared collection report** (`lint --all --prepare` over the denominator), for any config that has a `select` anywhere: it shows each candidate's prepared selection request, each piece and request that needs no answer, and every `needs_answer` entry, and it sends nothing;
- **an evidence print,** only for a plain kind in a config with no `select` anywhere, where printing sends nothing.

At development and later, also the report's file counts, results and filter answers. Never ask for, or make, an evidence print or run of a config whose selection hasn't passed its review: it dispatches that selection.

**The frontier.** Coverage is read at the frontier the stage has reached:
- **before the selection's first run:** the candidate denominator (every file in the denominator gives the selected kind its candidates, each with its prepared selection request) and the required inclusions (every piece a must-warn case needs is among those candidates). Downstream pieces and fields are under `needs_answer`: they are pending, and this frontier says nothing about them;
- **after the selection's answers are recorded,** from a prepared report continued with `--selections-from`: the selected pieces (every required inclusion was chosen) and field coverage (each downstream request holds the fields its questions read, every cut named). An entry still under `needs_answer` stays pending downstream coverage, never passed;
- **a plain kind:** pieces and fields over the whole denominator, from either source.

**Return:** a check record, in the review schema's shape.

**Complete when:** the chunk design and every item below were read against the config and the collection, at the stage's frontier, with every `needs_answer` entry listed as pending. Without an admitted collection source the check is `partial`, naming it; a `needs_answer` entry never completes a coverage item.

| item | fails when | bad control | smallest correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chunk named, with its edges | the design doesn't state the subject, the denominator, the one-hop edges or the budget, or the print shows a field the design doesn't name | a section rule whose design says "sections" but not which folders, or whether loaded files are read | state each part, and show it on the collection |
| chunk chosen for a reason | the rule judges every piece of a shape when most pieces of that shape aren't what its standard governs, with no selection or gate; or it asks a model to pick pieces whose shape already decides them | a product-workflow rule asked of every section of every general skill, heading-only sections and plain rationale included, when it governs only sections that carry procedural material | add a one-question selection on the kind (shipped), or a gate in the rule; drop a selection where the shape decides; a selection that needs more than one question waits for the engine, under `lint-contract` |
| a selection keeps its required inclusions | the selection's question asks for one form of the governed material (directions to the reader) while the rule's must-warn cases include others (worked examples, table rows, quotations); or its desk check or first run leaves a required inclusion rejected or uncertain; or its coverage is claimed before that run | "does this section tell the reader to carry out steps or commands?", which drops an owner-ruled warning: a third-person worked example of one terminal app's verification setup (`project-verification-selection.md` lines 39–42) | ask for every form of the material in the one question; desk-check every required inclusion; keep the broader mechanical kind, gated, until the run keeps them all |
| general skills only | a rule whose standard is for skills meant for any repository has no filter gate, or gates on a folder-name list, or on a filter whose own review isn't `ready` for this stage | `only_when` absent, so a product's own skill is judged as if it were general | gate with `only_when` on the kind filter's general, role and domain labels |
| unread is shown apart | the denominator isn't stated precisely enough to derive which files went unread, or a skipped folder, an unresolved selection candidate, a `needs_answer` entry or a cut piece would be reported as passed | a full run whose filter left nine folders unsure, reported as "no findings in those skills" | state the denominator; list each skipped, unresolved and cut piece as a gap |
| a misread fixed where collected | a piece misread because the kind collected the wrong text is repaired in a question's wording or criteria instead of in the evidence project | criteria told to "assume the options follow the lead-in" because the kind sent the lead-in without its list | fix the kind or field so the state holds the content; leave the question unchanged for that comparison |
