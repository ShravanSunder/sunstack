# Working with the Researcher

This is your side of commissioning a Researcher and adopting what it finds. `practices-manage-agents` owns its form (a Worker for one bounded question, a Sidekick for continuing work), model, route and packet. Return the question and the method you named, then each claim you adopted with the anchor you opened.

## Commission

Name the method in the commission's approach. Use `practices-how` for how current code behaves, `practices-why` for why it is that way, and `practices-research` for anything else. The Researcher routes to how or why only when the approach names it. Leave the method out and a why question runs as general research, without the history walk that separates a recorded reason from a guess.

Send the question as a question. If you suspect an answer, label it as a hypothesis for the Researcher to test. "Confirm that X" asks it to find support, and it usually will.

## Reading what comes back

The handback puts one claim on each line, tagged `source`, `history` or `inference` and ending with an anchor, then lists the gaps. Open the anchors behind the claims your decision rests on. Treat an untagged claim as inference until you have opened its anchor, and split a line that mixes two kinds before you use either half.

A `history` claim that the owner decided something needs an anchor in the owner's own words. An agent's account of the decision, an Advisor's read included, points you to where the decision may be recorded and decides nothing.

**Case:** three Researcher Workers were commissioned with `practices-why` as the method. Routing worked every time, and the labels never fully held. The third handback tagged most claims, left the summary answer untagged and mixed two kinds in one line. The summary answer is the line a Lead quotes, and quoting it as checked would have adopted a claim of unknown basis. **Clean case:** every claim carries its tag and anchor. You open the two anchors your decision rests on, find that they say what the claims say, and adopt those two claims.

## When to stop

Stop when every claim your decision rests on has an anchor you opened, and each gap says what was searched and what would settle it. If a gap blocks the decision, either commission the question that settles it or bring the gap to the owner. Don't commission another round on a question the sources already answered.
