# Research Countercheck

Give one independent checker the load-bearing claims, their primary anchors, exact questions, freshness requirement, and source coverage. Choose a route with history separate from the researcher's conclusions, and send only the claim and evidence brief. If the available route cannot preserve that boundary, record the countercheck gap. The checker returns per-claim evidence for the researcher's synthesis.

For each claim:

1. Reopen the primary anchor and identify what it directly supports.
2. Try to refute the claim with a concrete counterexample or contrary primary source.
3. Look for a competing hypothesis that explains the same observations.
4. Check freshness against live sources when the fact can drift.
5. Return `complete | partial | blocked`, the surviving or refuted claim, anchors both for and against, and the exact unfinished coverage or missing access.

The researcher compares this result with the original evidence before synthesis. A partial or blocked countercheck leaves the affected load-bearing conclusion unresolved unless another verified primary anchor settles the gap.
