# Give each form one job in a work account

This reference owns form selection for a work account or work-log page. Expected inputs: the account's reader questions, its supplied facts and any handover requirement the source states. Return: for each reader question, the selected form, its job and what stays in view.

An illustrative account asks: can this staged import be handed over? Its supplied facts are three of four stages verified locally, no release, a retry delay the author chose without measuring it, a real provider nobody has observed, and a proposed deletion a later note withdrew. This illustrative source also states its handover requirement: a rehearsal against the real provider before handover. Open with the answer that requirement gives: not yet, because the required provider rehearsal has not run. Then state the separate limits: three stages passed local checks, and nothing is released. A source that stated a different requirement would get the answer that requirement gives. Then give each reader question the form that does its job:

| Reader's question | Selected form and its job | What stays in view |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which stages are ready, and what blocks handover? | One native table with a row per stage; a documented progress part only for the supplied three-of-four count | Each stage's authored state, actual proof and next dependency in separate cells, and the no-release limit |
| What did the author decide or assume? | Short readable entries, or a table when the items share the same fields | The actual choice or belief, its reason and cost, who chose, the authority or its gap, and the work that depends on it |
| What changed for the consumer? | A keyed comparison, only for before/after topics the source supplies on both sides | What the change means and what it costs |
| What supports the conclusion? | A Trace, only for the supplied ordered finding, correction and check | Each claim's decisive excerpt, attribution and proof scope |
| Where is the full receipt? | A native disclosure beside its claim | The failure limit that governs the claim stays outside the disclosure |

The withdrawn deletion appears as withdrawn, not as an open proposal or a finished step. The retry delay is the author's unmeasured choice, not a measured latency, and the real provider stays unobserved: an integration evidence gap that three passing stages do not close.

Leave a form out when the facts cannot fill it. With an unknown number of stages, list the stages seen and omit the fraction; a single percentage across unrelated subjects, or against an overall total the source never supplied, claims a completeness nobody counted. Unrelated status facts need no forced comparison, and a single observation needs no manufactured sequence. The forms are alternatives with jobs, not a quota.

When a table cell holds a live component, such as a status badge or progress part, write a native HTML table, as the guide's small-parts recipe does. When the page displays literal markup or commands for inspection, use the guide's code carrier rather than live elements. The markup for each selected form comes from the matching guide through the existing binding route in [demistify.md](demistify.md); this example chooses forms and defines no syntax.

Complete when each reader question has a form whose job the supplied facts fill, and each form the facts cannot fill is left out.
