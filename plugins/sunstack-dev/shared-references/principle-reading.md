# Principle reading

**What:** principles (`principle-*`) are engineering judgment cards. **Why hidden:** they are hidden from model invocation so they don't crowd every context; a skill reads one at the decision that needs it.

**How:** a call to `principle-<name>` means read `../principle-<name>/SKILL.md` (from `shared-references/`: `../skills/principle-<name>/SKILL.md`) in full. Load its `references/example_scenarios.md` only when the principle says to. Your own full read earlier in this session counts; another agent's doesn't.

Use the result in your next step. Tell the owner only when it explains an outcome, risk or decision.
